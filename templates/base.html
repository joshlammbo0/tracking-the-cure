About this project

Tracking the Cure is an independent research project on the race to cure type 1 diabetes, built by someone who lives with it. It is not affiliated with any company it covers.

Not medical advice. Not investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to start, stop or change any treatment, or to buy or sell any security. Talk to your care team about your diabetes care. Notes labelled "My take" are the author's personal perspective; notes labelled "Analysis" are the author's interpretation of the cited data.
