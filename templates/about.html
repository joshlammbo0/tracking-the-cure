I live with type 1 diabetes, and I'm pursuing a career in healthcare investment banking. Those two facts meet in one question I kept asking and couldn't find a straight answer to: how close is a cure, really, and what happens to the companies built around managing the disease if it arrives?

Most coverage of the cure race either cheers every press release or ignores it. I wanted the view an analyst would build: every program graded on what it has actually shown, every number tied to a primary source, the open questions flagged rather than smoothed over, and a model that turns "what if" into dollars and companies.

Method in brief

  • Two grades per program. Stage reached versus evidence read out. The gap is where hype lives.
  • Live checks. ClinicalTrials.gov, openFDA, SEC EDGAR, PubMed and market data refresh daily. Disagreements with the curated record raise a flag.
  • A transparent model. The Cure Impact Model sizes displaced CGM, pump and insulin revenue under labelled assumptions, with sensitivities and a company read-across.
  • Sourced or labelled. Every fact cites a primary source; every judgment is labelled as an assumption, analysis or my take.
